# YouNeon growth mode

`GROWTH_MODE` in `lib/product-config.ts` is on.

- Matching and gender/country filters are free.
- Neon shop and Premium open **Coming soon** instead of Pi payment.
- Discover shows a live online count and honest copy.
- After a video match, that person stays unlocked in Messages (`rememberUnlockedPeer`).
- Flip `GROWTH_MODE` to `false` when the room is full and you want to charge again.
